# Alpaca Study Buddy

One line: A friendly alpaca turns pasted notes into encouraging quizzes and/or flashcards.

## The Unique Kernel

The alpaca is the emotional study companion. It welcomes the learner warmly and responds supportively to both correct and incorrect answers, so studying feels safe, cute, and motivating instead of like a pressured classroom quiz.

## Who It's For

Students who find ordinary notes boring and want to review them in a more engaging way, especially learners who feel pressure when using traditional quiz-like tools. The same simple note transformation may also help anyone who wants to review information, not only students.

## The Core Loop

The user opens the web app, pastes notes, chooses quiz, flashcards, or both, and asks the AI to process the notes in the selected mode. They review the generated material, answer quiz questions or turn through flashcards, and receive encouragement from the alpaca so they want to continue.

## Inspiration & Identity

Cute, warm, and emotionally supportive. The alpaca is the main character and should feel like a personal study companion rather than a childish or embarrassing classroom mascot. The visual theme should make the user feel happy and comfortable using it.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The learner loves alpacas and wants one to be a study companion. They want to improve their coding by implementing AI that understands what the user wants and produces an accurate, useful result rather than acting like a generic chatbot.

## What "Working" Looks Like

A user can paste a note, choose quiz, flashcards, or both, and receive study material that reflects the note. In quiz mode, the user can answer and see the alpaca cheer them on whether the answer is correct or incorrect. In flashcard mode, the user can review the generated cards. The compelling moment is seeing a boring pasted note become a usable study experience while the alpaca makes the learner feel encouraged rather than judged.

## The POC Boundary

In:

- A single web flow for pasting notes.
- A choice of quiz, flashcards, or both.
- AI generation based on the selected choice and the pasted note.
- A usable quiz review interaction.
- A usable flashcard review interaction.
- Alpaca welcome, correct-answer celebration, and supportive incorrect-answer response.
- A focused cute visual theme that makes the experience feel comfortable and motivating.

## Later

- Note summarization and conclusion generation.
- Turning notes into an organized plan list.
- Motivational quotes or a broader motivation system.
- More characters, including a bunny.
- A larger study history or personalization system.

## Explicitly Cut

- Summary and plan-list modes are not in the first proof of concept; they expand the AI output surface beyond the core study loop.
- Motivational quotes are not a first-version requirement; the alpaca's contextual encouragement is enough to test the emotional kernel.
- A full classroom, grading, or pressure-oriented assessment system is excluded because avoiding that feeling is central to the product.
